The Eiffel Tower is a wrought-iron lattice tower in Paris, France. It was designed by Gustave Eiffel and completed in 1889 for the World Fair. The tower stands 330 metres tall and was the tallest structure in the world until 1930.

The Louvre Museum is the largest art museum in the world, located in Paris on the Seine river. Its collection includes the Mona Lisa painted by Leonardo da Vinci. Gustave Eiffel also engineered the internal frame of the Statue of Liberty.
