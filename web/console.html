      The research

      The problem in AI

      Standard RAG treats memory as a flat bag of text chunks: embed everything, rank by cosine similarity, paste the top-k into the model. That has three structural failures: it loses structure (a fact and the concept it belongs to are just two equally-shaped vectors), it loses multi-hop reasoning (answering “the mentor of the director of film X” needs chains, not neighbors), and it wastes context on near-duplicate chunks.

      The hypothesis

      Inspired by the radial organization of mandalas, with a center, concentric rings of decreasing generality, and semantic sectors, MIRA arranges memory as a formal topology: ring 0 core, ring 1 major concepts, ring 2 subconcepts, ring 3 facts/evidence, ring 4 source documents. Every node carries a computed radial distance (semantic + hierarchical + graph + temporal distance). Retrieval scores nine components, including spreading activation, with energy that propagates through the graph the way activation spreads in associative memory (Collins & Loftus 1975).

      Honesty: MIRA is inspired by mandala organization principles and by biological memory dynamics. It does not claim mandalas were neural networks or that this simulates a brain. Whether radial organization helps is an empirical question: the system is built to answer it, including with “no”.

      Three biological timescales

      • Fast (per query): spreading activation propagates energy through the mandala to surface multi-hop associates that vector search misses.
      • Medium (per answer): nine-component scoring weighs semantic, structural, radial, graph, activation, confidence, recency and path evidence.
      • Slow (consolidation): accepted grounded answers can strengthen path edges; LIF spike timing adds bounded STDP-like potentiation/depression, and inactive edges decay. This is an engineering heuristic, not biochemical STDP.

      What it is used for

      • A private research memory over your own PDFs, notes and web pages. Grounded answers include document/page/chunk provenance; identity and parametric answers are labeled and do not fabricate sources.
      • An experimental laboratory: every mechanism (vector, graph, hierarchy, radial, activation) is individually switchable and benchmarkable.
      • A learning memory: a delta-rule unit learns the retrieval weights from your corpus; web-fetched pages become permanent memories; Hebbian consolidation rewires the graph toward what you actually ask.

      Sanity checks (not research conclusions)

      • The optional learned scorer is disabled by default; earlier single-corpus numbers are not held-out evidence.
      • Agent routing checks cover memory answers, consented web ingestion, and labeled ungrounded fallbacks.
      • Topology diagnostics verify placement, radial distance, and provenance plumbing.

      These checks validate implementation behavior, not general AI quality. Run the Benchmarks page on real datasets with exact evidence labels for research claims; the paper draft lives in paper/mira_paper.md.

      What problem this solves in AI, concretely

      • Context efficiency: compression keeps only answer-critical evidence, so a 1.5B model on 4 GB VRAM behaves like a much larger one on its own corpus.
      • Auditable grounding: grounded answers show retained evidence, paths, and provenance; identity and parametric answers are explicitly labeled and never receive fabricated citations.
      • Continual learning without fine-tuning: the memory grows (web ingest) and rewires (Hebbian) while the model stays frozen; external memory instead of expensive retraining.
      • Honest evaluation culture: ablations and baselines are first-class diagnostics; causal attribution requires frozen candidate controls.
